Q1 · FCA §841
At the conclusion of a dispositional hearing under Article 8 of the Family Court Act, the court may enter which orders?
1. Dismissing the petition if allegations are established  ·  2. Suspending judgment not exceeding 3 months  ·  3. Placing respondent on probation not exceeding 6 months  ·  4. Making an order of protection under §842  ·  5. Directing payment of restitution up to $10,000
✓ D — FCA §841 permits: an order of protection (§842) AND restitution up to $10,000. Cannot dismiss if allegations ARE established; probation/suspension limits differ from stated.
Q2 · FCA §427(a)
Personal service of a summons and petition by delivery to a suitable-age person at the place of business/dwelling, plus mailing, must be made at least how many days before the appearance date?
✓ A — FCA §427(a): At least EIGHT days before the appearance date.
Q3 · FCA §168.2
A copy of an order of protection issued by Family Court must be filed by the clerk with the sheriff or police department of the county where the ___ resides.
✓ A — FCA §168.2: Filed where the PETITIONER resides — not the respondent.
Q4 · FCA §312.1
A summons issued by Family Court upon the filing of a juvenile delinquency petition must be signed by:
1. The probation department  ·  2. The presentment agency  ·  3. The clerk of the court  ·  4. A judge of the court
✓ C — FCA §312.1: Signed by a JUDGE or by the CLERK OF THE COURT only.
Q5 · DRL §75-a
Which is NOT a "child custody proceeding" under the Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction Enforcement Act (DRL §75-a)?
✓ D — DRL §75-a expressly excludes: juvenile delinquency, PINS, and CONTRACTUAL EMANCIPATION.
Q6 · Uniform Rules 205.43(b)
A support magistrate must commence a hearing to determine willful violation of child support within ___ days of the summons date. The hearing must conclude within ___ days of commencement.
✓ D — Uniform Rules 205.43(b): Commence within 30 days; conclude within 60 days.
Q7 · CPL §500.10(9)
"Bail" means:
1. Cash bail or a bail bond  ·  2. Real property  ·  3. Equity in full force and effect
✓ A — CPL §500.10(9): "Bail" = cash bail, bail bond, or money paid with a credit card. Real property and equity are NOT included.
Q8 · CPL §320.10(2)
A defendant's waiver of jury trial in Superior Court must include:
1. May be made in writing  ·  2. Signed by defendant in person in open court in the presence of the court  ·  3. Consent of the prosecutor  ·  4. Approved by the court
✓ D — CPL §320.10(2): Must be IN WRITING, SIGNED BY DEFENDANT IN OPEN COURT, WITH COURT APPROVAL. Prosecutor consent NOT required.
Q9 · CPL §380.90(2)
When a student is sentenced for a crime, the court must notify the school's educational official. This applies to students up to and including age:
✓ C — CPL §380.90(2): "Person under the age of NINETEEN" = up to and including age 18.
Q10 · CPL §160.50(1)
When a criminal action is terminated in favor of a person, the DA or court must give the person or attorney how many days' notice before moving that interests of justice require otherwise (opposing sealing)?
✓ A — CPL §160.50(1): NOT LESS THAN FIVE DAYS notice to the person or attorney.
Q11 · CPL §720.20(1)
When must the court determine whether an eligible youth is a youthful offender?
✓ D — CPL §720.20(1): After receiving the pre-sentence report, the court determines YO status AT THE TIME OF PRONOUNCING SENTENCE.
Q12 · CPL §170.56(1)-(2)
Upon arraignment on marijuana possession/sale charges (§§221.05, 221.10, 221.15, 221.35, or 221.40), before a plea or trial, the court may adjourn in contemplation of dismissal for not more than:
✓ A — CPL §170.56(1)-(2): Total period shall not exceed TWELVE MONTHS.
Q13 · CPLR §3025(a)
A party may amend a pleading ONCE without leave of court:
1. Within twenty days after its service  ·  2. Within thirty days after its service  ·  3. Any time before response period expires  ·  4. Within thirty days after service of a responding pleading
✓ C — CPLR §3025(a): Within TWENTY days after service (1) or before response period expires (3). Options 2 and 4 say 30 days — wrong.
Q14 · CPLR §4402
The court may order a continuance or new trial in the interest of justice on motion of any party:
✓ B — CPLR §4402: "At any time DURING THE TRIAL."
Q15 · CPLR §1201
Unless the court appoints a guardian ad litem, an infant may be represented in court by:
1. The guardian of his property  ·  2. A parent that has legal custody  ·  3. An adult spouse who resides with the infant
✓ D — CPLR §1201: All three are valid representatives.
Q16 · CPLR §2304
A motion to quash, fix conditions, or modify a subpoena must be made in the:
✓ B — CPLR §2304: "Made promptly in the COURT IN WHICH THE SUBPOENA IS RETURNABLE."
Q17 · CPLR §5003
Every order directing payment of money, docketed as a judgment, shall bear interest from the:
✓ D — CPLR §5003: Interest from the DATE OF SUCH DOCKETING.
Q18 · Uniform Civil Rules 202.22(a)(7)
A "Military Calendar" is:
✓ D — Uniform Rules 202.22(a)(7): Party or witness "in military service, and is not presently available for trial."
Q19 · FCA §§216-c, 233
Pursuant to Article 2 of the FCA, which statements are correct?
1. Clerk and deputy clerk shall be in each county  ·  2. No clerk/probation officer may prevent petition filing  ·  3. Court may order medical care for a child in need  ·  4. Child with physical disabilities = under 18 expected to be incapacitated  ·  5. Original Family Court order filed with County Clerk
✓ B — FCA §216-c (no one may prevent petition filing) and FCA §233 (medical order for child in need) are correct.
Q20 · FCA §580-311
Regarding a UIFSA petition (FCA §580-311) to modify a child support order of another state, which is NOT correct?
✓ D — FCA §580-311: A copy of the other state's order is required UNLESS filed at the time of registration — not required in ALL cases.
Q21 · PL §70.05
A sentence of imprisonment for a felony committed by a juvenile offender shall be a(n):
✓ C — PL §70.05: "A sentence of imprisonment for a felony committed by a juvenile offender shall be an INDETERMINATE sentence."
Q22 · CPL §400.19(2)
To qualify as a second child sexual assault felony offender, the defendant must have been at least what age at the time of the predicate felony?
✓ C — CPL §400.19(2): The defendant must have been EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE OR OLDER at the time of the predicate felony.
Q23 · CPLR §3403(a)
Which civil actions are entitled to a calendar preference under CPLR §3403?
1. Interests of justice require early trial  ·  2. Party is 70 years of age  ·  3. Dental malpractice action  ·  4. Terminally ill plaintiff alleging defendant's negligence
✓ D — CPLR §3403(a): ALL FOUR qualify. Dental malpractice is covered under §3403(a)(5) (medical, dental, or podiatric malpractice).
Q24 · CPLR §2101(e)
Except where otherwise prescribed, copies rather than originals of which papers may be served or filed?
1. Affidavits  ·  2. Orders  ·  3. Exhibits  ·  4. Notes of Issue
✓ D — CPLR §2101(e): "Copies of ALL PAPERS, including orders, affidavits and exhibits may be served or filed." All four are covered.
