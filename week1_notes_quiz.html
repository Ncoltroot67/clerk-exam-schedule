📋 Study Notes — CPL Art. 1 (updated per official NY Legislature text)
§1.00–§1.10 Basics
§1.00CPL = Criminal Procedure Law
§1.10Applies exclusively to ALL criminal actions, appeals, and post-judgment proceedings
§1.20 — The 7 Accusatory Instruments
Filing of an accusatory instrument commences a proceeding. The 7 types:
📊 Quick Reference Chart — CPL 1.20

LCC = Local Criminal Court · SCI = Superior Court Information · DA = District Attorney · Sup. Dep. = Supporting Deposition
1Indictment (including reduced indictment)
2Information
3Simplified Information (3 subtypes: Traffic, Parks, Environmental Conservation)
4Prosecutor's Information
5Superior Court Information

🆕 Parking infraction appearance ticket (verified, with plate/type/expiration/make/model/body type) also qualifies [§1.20(1)(b)]
§1.20(2)Superior Courts: Indictments + Superior Court Information ONLY. All others = Local Criminal Courts.
§1.20(3–8) — Each Instrument
IndictmentBy GRAND JURY — at least one crime — Superior Court only
Superior Court InfoBy District Attorney — same force as indictment — Superior Court only
⚡ OFFENSE = any act punishable by fine/imprisonment. CRIME = misdemeanors + felonies ONLY
InformationVerified by individual — NO FELONIES — commences AND serves as basis for prosecution
Simplified InfoAll 3 types: commence AND serve as basis for prosecution
Prosecutor's InfoBy DA — non-felony offenses only (traffic infraction)
Misdemeanor ComplaintAt least one misdemeanor, no felonies — commences action — prosecution only upon defendant's consent
Felony ComplaintLocal Criminal Court — felonies only — commences action but NOT basis for prosecution
§1.20(9–18) — Key Procedural Definitions
Arraignment §1.20(9)Court acquires control over defendant — sets bail, assigns counsel, schedules proceedings
Jury Trial §1.20(11)Commences with jury selection. Non-jury trial = first opening address (or first witness if no address)
Conviction §1.20(13)When plea or verdict entered — EXCEPT on a Felony Complaint
Judgment §1.20(14-15)= Conviction + Sentence
Criminal Action §1.20(16-17)Commences with filing of first accusatory instrument
§1.20(26–30) — Process & Warrants
Appearance Ticket §1.20(26)By public servant — for instrument not yet filed
Summons §1.20(27)By local OR superior court — only after action commenced — for arraignment
Warrant of Arrest §1.20(28)Local Criminal Courts ONLY — executed by POLICE
Superior Court Warrant §1.20(29)Superior Court — same effect as warrant of arrest
Bench Warrant §1.20(30)For previously arraigned defendants — directed to police officer OR uniformed court officer §530.70(b)
§1.20(31–45) — People, Officers & Special Definitions
Prosecutor §1.20(31-32)= DA + Attorney General + deputies + Special Prosecutor + IG for Transportation
⚡ NYC Sheriffs = civil jurisdiction ONLY — NOT police officers under CPL
Petty Offense §1.20(39)= Violation OR Traffic Infraction (NOT misdemeanors)
Juvenile Offender §1.20(42)Age 13: Murder 2 or Sexually Motivated Felony only. Age 14-15: broader list. Age 14: firearm only if on school grounds
🆕 Adolescent Offender §1.20(44): Age 16 (on/after Oct 1 2018) or Age 17 (on/after Oct 1 2019) charged with felony — Youth Part (Raise the Age)
🆕 Expunge §1.20(45): Arrest deemed nullity — records marked expunged/destroyed per CPL §160.50
Lesser-Included §1.20(37)Cannot commit greater without simultaneously committing lesser. All attempts automatically lesser-included
